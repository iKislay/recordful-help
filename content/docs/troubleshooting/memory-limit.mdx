---
title: What does memory limit reached mean?
description: The device ran out of space, the recording stopped safely, and your video is kept.
---

The device ran out of storage mid-recording, so Recordful stopped automatically. Nothing captured is lost: the recording up to that point opens in the editor for download.

To record longer next time:

1. Free up disk space (Recordful needs 1 GB free to start).
2. Lower the quality: turn off Highest quality, or lower max resolution and FPS in settings.
3. Clear previous recordings from the popup options, or export them first.
4. On ChromeOS or small disks, prefer shorter takes and export between them.
