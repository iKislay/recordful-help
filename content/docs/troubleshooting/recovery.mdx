---
title: Recover a lost recording
description: Restore the last recording and download raw data when the editor looks empty.
---

- **Restore last recording:** the popup has a button that reopens the most recent capture.
- **Recovery mode:** if the editor loads without your video, it offers the raw video data for download. The raw file is the unprocessed capture: large, sometimes awkward to play, but complete.
- **Diagnostic report:** the editor's Help menu downloads system info plus recent session data. Send it to support with a description of what happened. No video or audio is included.
