title: Recover a lost recording
description: "If Chrome crashes, the power cuts out or the extension updates while you record, Recordful keeps what you recorded and offers it back."
---

## After a crash

Recordful saves your recording to your computer as it goes. If Chrome crashes, the computer loses power, or the extension updates in the middle of a recording, the part you already recorded is still there.

The next time you open Recordful, it asks: **Your last recording was interrupted**.

- **Recover recording** opens it in the editor, up to the moment it stopped. Export it or share it like any recording.
- **Discard** lets it go. Its space is freed the next time you record.

This works signed in or out, on every plan. If you close the question without choosing, the recording stays until your next recording, and **Restore last recording** in the popup's settings menu still finds it.

## Cloud recordings

A cloud recording (Pro) uploads while you record. If it is cut off, the upload picks up where it stopped when Chrome is back, and the recording appears in your library.

## If the editor looks empty

- **Recovery mode:** if the editor loads without your video, it offers the raw video data for download. The raw file is the unprocessed capture: large, sometimes awkward to play, but complete.
- **Diagnostic report:** the editor's Help menu downloads system info plus recent session data. Send it to support with a description of what happened. No video or audio is included.
