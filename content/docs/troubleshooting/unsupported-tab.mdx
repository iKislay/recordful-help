---
title: Extension not supported on this tab
description: Why the popup refuses some pages and how to record them anyway.
---

Chrome blocks extensions on the Web Store, `chrome://` pages, and some new-tab pages. Recordful cannot show the camera bubble or toolbar there.

Workaround: start the recording from a normal tab, then switch to the restricted page once capture has begun. Tab-audio capture is also disabled on those pages, so screen recording without tab audio is the fallback, and the popup says so with a toast.
