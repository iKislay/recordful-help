---
title: What changed in the new version
description: The new look, same free private recorder, plus Pro cloud features.
---

Recordful got a new look and new features, and the core promise is unchanged: free, private, open source, no watermarks, no limits on local recording.

- New popup and toolbar design.
- Built-in editor: trim, crop, mute, add audio, export to MP4, WebM, or GIF.
- Optional Pro: cloud storage with share links, multi-scene editing, auto-zoom on clicks, and captions.

Full history lives in the [changelog](https://recordful.app/changelog).
