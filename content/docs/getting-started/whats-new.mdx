The core promise is unchanged: free, private, open source, no watermarks, no limits on local recording.

## New in 4.10

- **Your AI app can edit your videos.** The [hosted connector](/docs/ai-agents/connector) at recordful.app/mcp grows from 7 read tools to 114: cuts and clips, silences and ums, zooms, captions and chapters, look, audio, shapes and blurs, motion graphics, frames and storyboards, search, tags, sharing, analytics and webhooks. Editing is part of Pro. [Tool reference](/docs/ai-agents/tools/editing)
- **The same tools in recordful-mcp 2.0**, through Recordful 4.10.0's own sign-in, plus four skills: edit-video, remove-mistakes, make-short and polish-demo.

## New in 4.9

- **Your recording survives a crash.** If Chrome crashes or the power cuts out, Recordful offers the recording back the next time you open it. [Learn more](/docs/troubleshooting/recovery)
- **Edit by transcript.** Delete words to cut them from the video, or find a phrase and remove every place you said it. [Learn more](/docs/editing/transcript)
- **Voice EQ** with five presets, from Natural to Broadcast. [Learn more](/docs/editing/voice-eq)
- **Shapes:** arrows, boxes, rings, highlights and numbered steps on the video. [Learn more](/docs/editing/shapes)
- **Markers:** flag moments while you record and jump to them in the editor. [Learn more](/docs/editing/markers)
- **Transitions, style presets, preview speed, save a frame as PNG**, and more. [Learn more](/docs/editing/presets-transitions)
- **Share pages:** a call to action, viewer invites by email, private by default, replacing a video behind the same link, and editable summaries. [Learn more](/docs/pro/share-links)
- **Move from Loom** with the importer. [Learn more](/docs/pro/loom-import)
- **Connect an AI app with no install** through the hosted connector. [Learn more](/docs/ai-agents/connector)

Full history lives in the [changelog](https://recordful.app/changelog). See the [blog](https://recordful.app/blog) for guides and comparisons.
