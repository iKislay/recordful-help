---
title: Allow camera and microphone access
description: Grant Chrome permission to use your camera and mic, and what to do when a site blocks them.
---

If Recordful shows "doesn't have permission", grant it once in Chrome:

1. Click the camera icon in the address bar.
2. Set Camera and Microphone to Allow for this site.
3. Reopen the Recordful popup and pick your devices again.

Some websites send a `Permissions-Policy` header that blocks all extensions from using the camera and microphone on that page. On those pages the recording still works, but the camera bubble stays hidden. Workaround: start the recording from another tab, then switch back to the page once recording has begun.

To test: open the popup, select a camera and microphone in the dropdowns, and check the preview bubble appears before you press record.
