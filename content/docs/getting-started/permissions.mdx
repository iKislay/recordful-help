---
title: Why does Recordful ask for permissions?
description: What each Chrome permission is used for and what Recordful never does with it.
---

Recording requires access to the thing being recorded. That is the whole list:

- **Capture your screen, tab, camera, and microphone.** Only used when you press record, to produce the video and audio you asked for.
- **Read the current page.** Only to draw the recording toolbar, camera bubble, countdown, and annotation canvas over the page you are recording.
- **Storage.** Keeps your recordings in the browser on your device so long videos work offline.
- **Downloads.** Saves the MP4, WebM, or GIF you export.
- **Keep the device awake.** Prevents sleep in the middle of a recording.

What Recordful does not do: no browsing history collection, no page content reading, nothing transmitted anywhere. Free recordings stay on your device unless you export them or, with Pro, upload them. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://recordful.app/privacy).
