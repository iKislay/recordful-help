---
title: Hide the toolbar while recording
description: Keep the toolbar visible, hide it on hover, or hide it completely.
---

While recording, the floating toolbar gives you pause, stop, restart, drawing, blur, camera, and mic controls.

- **Hide toolbar when not in use:** the toolbar fades out and reappears when your cursor approaches it.
- **Stay in the page when recording:** keeps you on the page instead of moving to the recorder tab. Camera and toolbar overlays may be hidden on pages Chrome restricts.
- To bring a hidden toolbar back, reopen the popup and re-enable it in options.
