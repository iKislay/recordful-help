---
title: Countdown and time limits
description: Use the countdown to settle in, and alarms to stop on time.
---

- **Countdown:** a short delay before capture starts. Click anywhere to skip it.
- **Set a time limit:** the popup can stop the recording automatically after a duration you choose.
- Recordings stop on their own at the 90-minute mark. You get a warning toast near the end, and everything up to that point is kept.
