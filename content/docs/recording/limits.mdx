---
title: Recording length and quality
description: How long you can record, quality settings, and what affects them.
---

- No fixed recording cap besides the 90-minute automatic stop and your free disk space. Keep 1 GB free at minimum.
- **Highest quality** toggle in the popup records at a higher bitrate and uses more disk.
- **Max resolution and max FPS** in settings cap what the recorder asks Chrome for. If a resolution is greyed out, your display cannot produce it.
- If the device runs out of space mid-recording, the recording stops and everything captured so far is kept and opened in the editor.
