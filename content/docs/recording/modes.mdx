---
title: What you can record
description: Tab, screen, camera, and custom area, and when to use each.
---

Open the popup and pick one:

- **Tab area.** Just the current tab, with optional tab audio. Best for demos and meetings.
- **Screen.** The whole monitor, an app window, or a region. Pick it in the Chrome share picker after pressing record.
- **Camera.** Camera only, for talking-head clips.
- **Custom area.** Drag a rectangle to record part of the page (needs Chrome 104+).

Keyboard shortcuts work while recording: Alt+Shift+G starts, Alt+Shift+X cancels, Alt+Shift+M pauses or resumes.
