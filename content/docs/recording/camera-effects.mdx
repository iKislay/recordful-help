---
title: Camera bubble and background effects
description: Show the camera while recording, flip it, blur the background, and annotate live.
---

- Pick a camera in the popup to show the bubble over the recording. Flip it from options if mirrored.
- **Background effects** blur or replace the camera background while you present.
- While recording: draw with pen, highlighter, arrows, shapes, and text; blur sensitive regions; spotlight the cursor or highlight clicks to guide attention.
