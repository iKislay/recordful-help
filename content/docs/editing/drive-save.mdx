---
title: Save to Google Drive
description: Send the finished video to Drive after signing in with Google.
---

In the editor, choose Save to Drive and sign in with Google when asked. The current version uploads to your Drive and you can sign out from the same button.

Trouble signing in usually means the OAuth client needs replacing with your own ID (self-hosted builds) or a fresh sign-in. Contact support if the button spins without finishing.
