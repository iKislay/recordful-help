---
title: Cut, trim, or mute parts of your video
description: Select a section on the timeline, then trim, cut, or mute it.
---

1. Stop the recording to open the editor.
2. Drag the handles on the timeline to select a section.
3. Choose **Trim video** (keep only the selection), **Cut section** (remove it), or **Mute audio** (silence it).

Undo and redo are in the editor toolbar. Revert to original restores the raw capture.
