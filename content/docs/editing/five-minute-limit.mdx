---
title: Can I edit long recordings?
description: Recordings of any length open in the full editor; when a long one opens for playback only.
---

Recordful edits on your device, so recordings stay private. On most computers, Chrome's fast recorder is used, and every recording opens in the full editor whatever its length.

If Chrome can't use the fast recorder on your computer, a recording longer than 7 minutes opens for playback only. You can still watch it and download the WebM file. Shorter recordings open in the full editor as usual.
