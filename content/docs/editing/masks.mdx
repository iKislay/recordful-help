---
title: Blur or spotlight part of a recording
description: "Hide an email, a password or a name with a blur mask, or dim everything around one area with a spotlight, in the web editor and the desktop app."
---

A **blur** hides what is under its box. A **spotlight** dims everything around its box, to draw the eye to one part of the screen. Both stay on target when the video zooms or is cropped.

![The web editor's Masks tool with a blur box over part of a page.](/masks-blur.png)

## In the web editor

1. Open a recording in the editor and move the playhead to where the mask should start.
2. Click **Masks** in the left rail, or press **9**.
3. Click **Add blur** or **Add spotlight**. The mask lands at the playhead.
4. Drag the box on the picture to place it, and its corners to size it.
5. Drag the mask on the **Masks** row of the timeline to change when it starts and ends. Switch between **Blur** and **Spotlight** at the top of the panel, or delete it with the bin.

Where a browser can't blur, a blur mask becomes a coarse mosaic instead, so nothing under it shows through. Masks are saved with the edit, including a cloud recording's edit, and are in the exported video.

## In the desktop app

In the desktop app, masks are annotations:

1. Add an annotation: press **A**, or click **Annotate** above the timeline.
2. In its settings, pick **Blur** or **Spotlight**.
3. Drag the box on the preview to place it, and the annotation on the timeline to time it.

## Good to know

- A mask covers the box for its whole time on the timeline. To follow something that moves, add a second mask where it goes.
- The original recording is never changed. The mask is part of the edit and the export.
