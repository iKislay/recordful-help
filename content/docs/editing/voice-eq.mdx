title: Tune your voice with the Voice EQ

The **Voice EQ** shapes how your voice sounds: less boom, more clarity, a warmer or a broadcast tone. It turns on with [Recordful Pro](/docs/pro/free-vs-pro) or any [AI credits](/docs/pro/ai-credits) left, and spends none.

## Turn it on

1. Open a recording in the editor and click **Audio** in the left rail.
2. Under **Voice EQ**, turn on **Equalizer**.
3. Pick a **Preset**:

| Preset | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Natural | A light touch: trims rumble and a little mud, a hint of presence |
| Clear | Cuts boom and mud, lifts the words so they cut through |
| Warm | Fuller low end, softer top, for a close, relaxed voice |
| Bright | More air and sparkle, for a thin or dull microphone |
| Broadcast | Radio-style: deep, present and even |

## Set it yourself

Move the **high-pass** slider to remove rumble below a pitch, or any of the four bands to boost or cut it. The curve shows the result, and the preset changes to **Custom**. You hear each change at once while the preview plays.

## Good to know

- With **Noise and echo** on, the EQ shapes the cleaned voice, so use both together.
- The EQ is saved with the edit and is in the exported video. The original recording is never changed.
