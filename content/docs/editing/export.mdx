---
title: Export to MP4, WebM, or GIF
description: Pick a format and download the finished video to your device.
---

In the editor, open Export:

- **MP4** (recommended): plays everywhere.
- **WebM**: smaller, always available.
- **GIF**: capped at 30 seconds. Trim the clip first if the button refuses.

The file downloads through Chrome to your usual download location. If you set up backup folders in options, copies are kept there too.
