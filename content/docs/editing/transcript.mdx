---
title: Edit a video by editing its words
description: "Delete words from the transcript to cut them from the video, bring them back with a click, or find a phrase and remove every place you said it."
---

The editor can show everything you said as text. Deleting words there cuts them from the video, and the cut lands in the quiet between words, so nothing sounds clipped. Part of [Recordful Pro](/docs/pro/free-vs-pro).

## Open the transcript

1. Open a recording in the editor.
2. Click **Transcript** at the top of the editor, next to **Agent**. It opens on the right, beside the preview.
3. The first time, click **Make transcript**. Only the sound is sent to be transcribed, and it is deleted once heard.

## Cut words

- **Click a word** to play from it.
- **Select words** by dragging across them, or click one and Shift-click another, then press **Delete** or click **Delete words**.
- **Bring a word back** by clicking it while it is struck through.
- A cut you make on the timeline shows as struck-through words here, and the other way round. Undo works for both.

## Find and remove every match

Type a word or phrase in **Find words**, then click **Remove all**. Every place you said it is cut at once, for example each "you know" or a product name you said wrong.

## Filler words

**Audio → Filler words** cuts ums and uhs the same way, so they show struck through in the transcript too. Turning it off brings them all back.

## Good to know

- The original recording is never changed: cuts are part of the edit and the export.
- The transcript works on the recording's voice. A recording with no speech shows "No speech was heard".
