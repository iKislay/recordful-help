title: Add arrows, boxes and numbered steps
description: "Point at a button, box a setting or number the steps of a tutorial with shapes that appear on the video for as long as you choose."
---

Shapes draw on top of the recording: an arrow to point, a box or a ring to frame, a highlight to mark a line, and numbered steps for a walkthrough.

## Add a shape

1. Open a recording in the editor and move the playhead to where the shape should appear.
2. Click **Shapes** in the left rail.
3. Pick **Arrow**, **Line**, **Box**, **Ring**, **Highlight** or **Step**. It lands at the playhead.
4. On the picture, drag a box or a ring by its corners to place it. Drag an arrow or a line by its ends, or by its middle to move it.
5. On the **Shapes** row of the timeline, drag the shape to change when it shows, and its ends to trim it.

## Style it

In the panel set the colour, line width, fill and opacity, the arrow's head (filled, open or double), and how it comes in: **fade**, **grow** or **draw on**, where the line draws itself.

## Good to know

- Shapes stay on their spot when the video zooms or is cropped, and look the same in the export and on the share page.
- Steps number themselves 1, 2, 3 in the order you add them.
- The original recording is never changed. Shapes are part of the edit.
