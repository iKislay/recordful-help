The Recordful Agent is an AI editor in a sidebar beside your video. Say what you want, and it makes each change in the editor while you watch. It is in beta and part of Pro.

## Open it

Open a recording in the editor at recordful.app, then press **Shift+S** or click the sparkle button in the top bar. Press **Shift+S** again, or the **×**, to close it.

## What to ask

- "Cut the filler words and long pauses."
- "What is this video about?"
- "Remove the part where I talk about pricing."
- "Zoom in on the first 5 seconds."
- "Add captions, a bit bigger, at the top."
- "Make it 9:16 with a dark gradient background for a Short."
- "Undo that."

The agent can:

- **Clean up speech.** Cut filler words (um, uh, a "like" that says nothing) and shorten long pauses, or put them back. These are the same as the **Filler words** and **Long pauses** switches under **Clean up**.
- **Cut a stretch.** Remove a section by its time, or by its topic: it reads the transcript first and says which lines it will cut.
- **Zoom.** Add a zoom for a stretch of the video, following your pointer where its path was recorded, or remove all zooms.
- **Captions.** Add captions (in a language you name, or the one it hears), remove them, or change their size and position.
- **Frame.** Change the padding, rounded corners, shape (Auto, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or 9:16) and background (none, a colour or a gradient).
- **Read the video.** Its length, clips and transcript.
- **Undo.** Like **Ctrl+Z**.

It can't yet change layouts, add titles, b-roll, music or motion graphics, or export. Ask and it will say so instead of pretending.

## Undo and stop

Every change the agent makes lands in the editor's history, so **Ctrl+Z** undoes it like an edit by hand. While it works, the send button turns into **Stop**.

## Credits

Each request spends a few of your workspace's AI credits, usually around 10. The number left shows at the top of the sidebar. See [AI credits](/docs/pro/ai-credits).

## Good to know

- The chat lasts while the editor is open. Closing the editor starts a fresh chat next time.
- The agent never makes up footage or words. Times it mentions come from your video.
- It runs in the web editor. The desktop app's editor gets it later.
