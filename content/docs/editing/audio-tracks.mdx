---
title: Add your own audio
description: Upload an audio file to replace or sit alongside the recorded sound.
---

In the editor, open the audio panel and drop in a file or click to browse. Set the volume, preview it against the video, then Update. **Replace existing audio** swaps the recorded sound out entirely.

Audio edits on clips longer than about 15 minutes are refused to protect the original. Trim first, then re-apply the edit.
