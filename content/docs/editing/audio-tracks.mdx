---
title: Add your own audio or a voiceover
description: Put music or another audio file under your video, or talk over it with your microphone after recording.
---

On the timeline, put the playhead where the sound should start, then click the **+** beside **Audio** and choose:

- **Upload an audio file**: music or any other sound, such as MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG or WebM. It starts at the playhead and plays for as long as it lasts, up to the next sound already there.
- **Record with microphone**: a voiceover. The first time, allow the microphone when your browser asks. The video plays from the playhead, muted so it is not picked up, while you talk. Press the same button (now a stop square) when you are done, or pause, or let the video reach its end. The take lands on the timeline where you spoke it.

Select a sound on the timeline to set its volume, or drag its ends to trim it. Delete removes it.

If the microphone is refused, allow it for recordful.app in Chrome's site settings (the icon left of the address), then try again.

## In the desktop app

**Add audio** is in the timeline's toolbar and offers the same two choices. If the microphone does not work, check it is allowed for Recordful in your system's privacy settings.
