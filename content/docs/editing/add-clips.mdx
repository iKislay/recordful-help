---
title: Add clips and change their order
description: Combine recordings into one video, continue an earlier project, and drag clips into any order.
---

To continue a video or combine several, add clips to it in the editor.

1. On the timeline, click the **+** beside **Video**.
2. Choose where the clip comes from:
   - **From Recordful**: your other recordings and screenshots, in this browser and in your cloud library. Pick one.
   - **From your computer**: one or more files. Videos can be MP4, WebM or MOV; pictures can be PNG, JPG or WebP.
3. The clip goes at the end of your video. A screenshot or picture shows for 3 seconds; drag its end on the timeline to make it longer or shorter.

Keep the tab open while a clip is added. Long videos take a moment to join.

## Change the order of clips

Drag a clip along the **Video** track and drop it before or after another. A blue line shows where it will land. Zooms, captions, muted parts and music stay with the part of the video they belong to.

To split one recording into clips you can move, put the playhead where you want the cut and press **Split** (or **S**).

## In the desktop app

**Add clip** is in the timeline's toolbar. **From Recordful** opens your Videos, where you drag or double-click a recording onto the timeline. **From your computer** takes the same videos and pictures, plus MKV and AVI. Drag clips on the timeline to change their order.
