## Transitions between clips

Where two clips meet, the timeline shows a small dot. Click it and pick **Crossfade** (the end of one clip fades into the next) or **Fade through black**. Pick **None** to cut straight. Each cut has its own transition, and it plays in the preview and the export.

## Style presets

A preset is your look saved under a name: background, padding, corners and shadow, camera layout, zoom depth, click effects and caption style.

1. Set up a recording the way you like it.
2. Click **Presets** at the top of the editor, then **Save current look**, and name it.
3. To use it on another video, open **Presets** and pick it. Undo takes it back.
4. Star a preset to make it the **default**: every new recording opens with that look.

Presets are kept in this browser.

## Watch your edit faster

The speed button under the preview (it shows **1×**) plays the preview at 0.5× up to 3×. It only changes how you watch; the video's own speed stays as it is. To change a clip's speed in the video, right-click the clip.

## Save a frame as a PNG

Click the **camera** button under the preview. The frame showing now is saved as a PNG at the video's full size, with your background, zooms, shapes and captions.

## Keep only one clip

Right-click a clip and pick **Delete other clips** to keep only that clip (or the clips you selected) and drop the rest in one step.

## Make auto-zoom your own

Open **Zoom** in the left rail to set how long a zoom **holds** after its last click, and how close together clicks must be to share one zoom (the **merge gap**). New recordings use these settings. To remake this video's zooms with them, click **Redo zooms from clicks**.
