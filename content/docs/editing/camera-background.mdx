---
title: Remove or blur your camera background (no green screen)
description: "Remove, blur or replace the background behind you in your camera video with a colour or an image, with no green screen. It's worked out on your own computer and free on every plan."
---

Recordful can take away the room behind you in your camera video, after you record. You don't need a green screen. Pick one of these:

- **Remove**: only you are shown, over the screen recording or the video's background.
- **Blur**: your room stays but goes soft, with a **Strength** slider.
- **Colour**: a plain colour behind you. Pick one of the swatches or any colour.
- **Image** (**Picture** in the desktop app): one of the built-in pictures, or your own.

## In the web editor

1. Open a recording that has a camera video in the editor.
2. Click **Camera** in the left rail.
3. Under **Background**, pick **Remove**, **Blur**, **Colour** or **Image**. **Original** puts your room back.

The first time, the editor prepares the cut-out. Keep the tab open while it does: on a typical laptop it takes a few seconds for every second of camera, and the panel shows how far along it is. It's saved with the recording, so it only happens once, and switching between Remove, Blur, Colour and Image after that is instant.

The background change shows in the preview and the export, and on a shared video or an embed.

## In the desktop app

1. Select your camera and open **Webcam** settings.
2. Under **Camera background**, pick **Remove**, **Blur**, **Colour** or **Picture**. **Original** puts your room back.

The cut-out is prepared the same way and kept with the project.

## Ask the Agent

With [the Recordful Agent](/docs/editing/agent) (Pro), say what you want:

- "Remove my background and use a blue colour."
- "Blur my background a bit."
- "Put NEXT UP behind me when I say next up."

## Text behind you

A title can sit behind your head, like a magazine cover. You don't need to remove your background for this: Recordful knows where you are in each frame, so the words go behind you and your room stays as it is. See [Motion](/docs/editing/motion#text-behind-you).

## Good to know

- **Nothing is uploaded for it.** The cut-out is worked out on your computer's graphics chip, the same way exporting works. It's free on every plan.
- **Things you hold.** Something you hold up, like a mug or a phone, can be treated as background and disappear with it. Hold it close to your body, or use **Blur** instead.
- **Camera-only recordings.** In the web editor, the background effects work when your camera is shown over a screen recording or full screen in a screen take.
- **Which browsers.** You prepare the cut-out in Chrome or Edge, or in the desktop app. People watching your shared video see the result in any browser.
