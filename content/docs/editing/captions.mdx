Captions are made from what you say, timed to each word. Most people watch Shorts, Reels and LinkedIn videos muted, so they matter most there.

![The Captions panel with the Boxed style, the spoken word on a yellow box](/captions-styles.png)

## Make them

1. Open **Captions** in the editor's left rail.
2. Pick the **Language**, or leave it on **Detect**.
3. Click **Generate captions**. Only the sound is sent, and it's deleted once heard. Generating captions uses AI credits.

Fix a misheard word by clicking its line in the list, or on the timeline.

## Pick a style

Each tile plays its look. Click one to use it; your size and position stay as they are.

| Style | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Classic** | A line on a dark plate. |
| **Karaoke** | Bold, outlined, the spoken word in colour, two lines at a time. |
| **Boxed** | Bold capitals, the spoken word on a box that slides from word to word. The Shorts look. |
| **One word** | One big word at a time, popping in as it's said. |
| **One word, colour** | The same, in colour. |
| **Reveal** | The whole line on a plate, the words still to come dimmed. |

## Change any part of it

- **Size**, and **position**: Top, Middle, **Shorts** (above the bottom, clear of the buttons TikTok, Shorts and Reels put there) or Bottom.
- **Word being spoken**: none, **Colour**, **Box** or **Dim rest**, and the colour it's in.
- **One word at a time**, or how many **lines** show at once. A long line shows a part at a time.
- **Capitals**, the **animation** (fade, rise, pop), a dark **background** or an outline, the **font** (Bold is a heavy display face) and the text colour.

## Good to know

- The highlight follows the word as it was said. If you rewrite a line so it has more or fewer words, it's timed by the length of each word instead.
- Captions are drawn into your export exactly as the editor shows them. **Caption file (.vtt)** downloads them as plain captions instead, for YouTube or LinkedIn.
- AI agents can set the same styles: the [Recordful Agent](/docs/editing/agent) in the editor, and `set_caption_style` with `preset` on the [connector](/docs/ai-agents/tools/captions).
