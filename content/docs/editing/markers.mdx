---
title: Mark moments while you record
description: "Drop a marker while recording, then find it on the editor's timeline to jump straight to that moment."
---

A marker is a flag on the timeline at a moment you want to find again: where the demo really starts, a mistake to cut, a result worth zooming into.

## While you record

- Click the **flag** on the recording toolbar. A "Marker added" note confirms it.
- Or set a keyboard shortcut for **Add a marker** at `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`, so you can mark without moving the mouse.

Time spent paused doesn't count, so a marker lands where it belongs in the video.

## In the editor

- Markers show as flags on the timeline's ruler. **Click one** to play from there.
- **Rename** a marker ("Signed in", "Bug shows") or **delete** it from the same flag.
- Press **Shift+M** to add a marker at the playhead.

## Good to know

- A marker follows its moment through cuts, speed changes and reordering. If you cut its part out, it hides, and comes back if you restore that part.
- Markers come with both local and cloud recordings.
