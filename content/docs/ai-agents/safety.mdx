## What your AI can and cannot do

- **Off until you turn it on**, and off again with one switch in **More → AI agents**.
- **Local only.** The server listens on `127.0.0.1`, so other devices and web pages can't reach it. A recording leaves your computer only when it is uploaded to recordful.app (Pro).
- **Its own tabs only.** Your AI acts only in tabs it opened itself, the tab you handed it by clicking the Recordful icon, and the tab being recorded. The tab it's using shows a chip with its name and a **Stop** button. Your other tabs stay out of reach.
- **Paired once per computer** after you approve a matching 4-digit code. Only the official Recordful extension can connect by default.
- **No credentials.** Uploads and transcripts use Recordful's own sign-in. Your AI never sees a token, and shouldn't ask you for one.
- **It asks first.** Good agents show you their plan and ask before recording and before uploading. Tell yours to if it doesn't.
- **Changes need your yes.** Renaming, changing who can watch, commenting, moving, creating folders and deleting all happen as you, so a good agent shows you the exact change first. Comments are public to everyone who can watch. Deleting needs the exact video named and an explicit confirmation, and can't be undone.
- **Desktop screenshots are off by default.** Your AI can screenshot your screen or a window only after you turn on **Let AI agents take screenshots** in the desktop app's **Settings → AI agents**, and only while the app is running. Each one shows a notification naming the AI, and the picture stays with you: it opens in the app's editor and never goes back to the AI. Turn the switch off to stop it.
- **Test data only.** Agents use test accounts and data. Sign in yourself on the tab first if a demo needs a real account.

## Revoke access

- In Recordful, **More → AI agents → Paired AI apps → Forget**.
- Or from a terminal: `npx -y recordful-mcp unpair --all`.
- Or turn the **AI agents** switch off.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Recordful isn't connected" | Click the Recordful icon once. Still nothing? Check **More → AI agents** is on. If it was on before Recordful 4.7.0, turn it off and on once so it can reconnect by itself |
| The AI shows a 4-digit code | Check it matches Recordful's window, click **Allow**, tell the AI to continue |
| `PERMISSIONS_NEEDED` | Recordful needs one-time permission to record. In **More → AI agents** turn the switch off and on again and click **Allow** in Chrome's prompt (or press Start recording in Recordful once) |
| Recording won't start | In tab or tab-area mode, click the Recordful icon on the tab being recorded, then retry |
| Recording waits forever | Recordful may be asking something: microphone access, or the screen picker in screen mode. Answer it. Tab and area modes need no picker |
| `PRO_REQUIRED` or `NOT_SIGNED_IN` | Uploads and transcripts need a Recordful Pro account, signed in in Recordful. Recording works on every plan |
| `BRIDGE_UNAVAILABLE` | Something other than recordful-mcp is using port 47823, or an older recordful-mcp is running. Update or close it |
| The AI says to turn on screenshots in the desktop app | Open the desktop app's **Settings → AI agents** and turn on **Let AI agents take screenshots** |
| The AI says the desktop app isn't running | Open the Recordful desktop app (2.0.0 or newer), then ask again |
| A window screenshot picks the wrong window | Ask your AI to run `list_windows` (or `recordful-mcp windows`) and use a more exact part of the title |
| No Recordful tools in the AI app | Check `node -v` is 20.10 or newer, then restart the app. Until it restarts the AI can use the [command line](/docs/ai-agents/cli) |

Run `npx -y recordful-mcp status` to see the connection and pairing state.
