Ask your AI to "record how the new sign-up flow works". It plans a short walkthrough, asks you to click the Recordful icon on the tab, then navigates, clicks and types with a smooth on-screen cursor while Recordful records. When it stops, Recordful's editor opens with the video. With Recordful Pro it can also upload the video, hand you the link and read the transcript back.

It runs on your computer: nothing to host, no account, no API key. The AI never needs a browser of its own, because Recordful's tools do the clicking.

Looking for the AI that edits inside Recordful's editor? That's the [Recordful Agent](/docs/editing/agent).

## What your AI can do

| Goal | How | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record a feature end to end | Start, pause, resume and stop, with page actions in between | Free and Pro |
| Drive the page | Navigate, read the page, click, type, press keys, scroll, wait, screenshot | Free and Pro |
| Take a screenshot | A Recordful screenshot of the tab (what's showing, or the whole page), opened in the screenshot editor in your usual look, or in 3D or another look when you ask | Free and Pro |
| Screenshot your computer | With the desktop app: the whole screen, one window or an area, opened in the app's editor | Free and Pro |
| Share | Upload a recording and return the link | Pro |
| Understand | Read back what was said (text or VTT) | Pro |
| Find videos | List your videos and check an upload's progress | Pro |
| Manage | Rename, set who can watch, organise into folders, check storage, delete | Pro |
| Collaborate | Read views, likes and comments; like and comment as you | Pro |

Ask for a screenshot in plain words: "screenshot the pricing page", "take a 3D screenshot of the whole page". The picture never goes back to the AI, so it costs only a few tokens, and the AI's cursor and Stop chip stay out of it. Needs Recordful 4.6.33 and recordful-mcp 0.8.0 or newer.

A new screenshot starts on a wallpaper photo picked to match its colours, unless you turned on **Remember my look**. A look your AI asks for is applied on top.

With the [desktop app](/docs/desktop/screenshots) 2.0.0 and recordful-mcp 1.0.0, your AI can also screenshot your whole screen, one window or an area, outside Chrome: "screenshot the Figma window", "screenshot my second monitor". Turn it on once in the desktop app first; see [Desktop screenshots](#desktop-screenshots) below.

On the free plan your AI records as usual and tells you once that links and transcripts come with Pro. It never fails for that reason.

## Two ways in

- **MCP server.** Your AI app runs `npx -y recordful-mcp` and gets the tools. Works in Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, opencode, Codex, Gemini CLI, Antigravity and any app that supports the Model Context Protocol.
- **Command line.** Anything with a shell can use `recordful-mcp start`, `stop`, `screenshot`, `upload`, `transcript` and more, with `--json` for machine-readable output. See [Command line](/docs/ai-agents/cli).

## Get started in two minutes

1. Install Recordful from the Chrome Web Store.
2. Add Recordful to your AI app: [paste one prompt](/docs/ai-agents/setup-prompt) and let the AI do it, or follow [Set up your AI app](/docs/ai-agents/setup).
3. In Recordful's popup, open **More → AI agents** and turn it on. Chrome asks to allow screen recording once; click **Allow**.
4. Ask your AI to record something. When it asks, click the Recordful icon on the tab, and approve the 4-digit pairing code the first time.

## Why you click the icon

Chrome only lets an extension record a tab after a person has clicked it there, and no app can click for you. The click also marks the one tab your AI may act in. Everything else is up to your AI.

## Desktop screenshots

1. Open the desktop app's **Settings → AI agents** and turn on **Let AI agents take screenshots**. It's off until you do, and turning it on is the one-time allow.
2. Keep the desktop app running and ask your AI for a screenshot of a window, a monitor or the whole screen.

Each screenshot shows a notification naming the AI that took it, and opens in the app's editor. No image goes back to your AI.

For the AI, `take_screenshot` takes these extra options:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `source` | `"screen"`, `"window"` or `"area"`. Leave it out to screenshot the Chrome tab as before |
| `window` | Part of a window's title or app name, for `source: "window"` |
| `display` | Which monitor: `1`, `2`, … or `"main"` |
| `look` | A look to apply, such as `"3d"` |

A new tool, `list_windows`, lists the windows your AI can pick from. On the command line: `recordful-mcp screenshot --source window --window Figma` and `recordful-mcp windows` (see [Command line](/docs/ai-agents/cli)).
