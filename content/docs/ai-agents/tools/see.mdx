Look at a video before editing it: frames, a storyboard, where the pointer went, and a preview link. 4 tools.

## `get_storyboard`

**Pro.** Reads only. 16 evenly spaced pictures of a Recordful video (or of start to end) as one 4 by 4 contact sheet, read left to right and top to bottom, with the time of each. The screen recording as recorded, before background, zoom and camera. The first call may answer not_ready while the pictures are taken: call again after retryAfterSeconds. Then get_video_frame looks closer at one moment.

**Parameters**

| Parameter | Type | Required | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `videoId` | string | yes | The video's id (videoId from list_videos). |
| `start` | number | no | Where to begin (default 0), in seconds of the video as it plays now. |
| `end` | number | no | Where to end (default the end), in seconds of the video as it plays now. |


**Returns** `videoId`, `version` (The edit's version. Pass it as expectedVersion to apply edits only to the edit you read), `start`, `end`, `frames` (Each picture's moment, in the sheet's order), `columns`, `rows`, `width`, `height`.

## `get_video_frame`

**Pro.** Reads only. A picture of a Recordful video at one moment, as a JPEG at most 1280 px wide: what the screen recording shows then, before the edit's background, zoom and camera are drawn over it. The first call for a moment may answer not_ready while the picture is taken: call again after retryAfterSeconds. Use get_storyboard first to find the moment.

**Parameters**

| Parameter | Type | Required | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `videoId` | string | yes | The video's id (videoId from list_videos). |
| `time` | number | yes | The moment, in seconds of the video as it plays now. |


**Returns** `videoId`, `version` (The edit's version. Pass it as expectedVersion to apply edits only to the edit you read), `time` (The moment shown, kept to a tenth of a second), `width`, `height`.

## `get_mouse_events`

**Pro.** Reads only. Where the pointer moved and clicked while a Recordful video was recorded, as the video plays now (cut parts left out): &#123; time, type: "click" | "move", x, y &#125;, with x and y as fractions of the recorded picture (0 to 1, a little outside for a window's edge). Up to 2000 in order; truncated means there are more, so narrow start and end. Recordings made before the pointer's path was kept have clicks only, and camera-only ones have none.

**Parameters**

| Parameter | Type | Required | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `videoId` | string | yes | The video's id (videoId from list_videos). |
| `start` | number | no | From (default 0), in seconds of the video as it plays now. |
| `end` | number | no | To (default the end), in seconds of the video as it plays now. |


**Returns** `videoId`, `version` (The edit's version. Pass it as expectedVersion to apply edits only to the edit you read), `start`, `end`, `events`, `truncated`.

## `get_live_preview_link`

**Pro.** Reads only. Links that play a Recordful video as edited now, from a moment: its page on recordful.app and its embeddable player. Who can open them follows the video's sharing settings.

**Parameters**

| Parameter | Type | Required | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `videoId` | string | yes | The video's id (videoId from list_videos). |
| `time` | number | no | Where it starts playing (default 0), in seconds of the video as it plays now. |


**Returns** `videoId`, `time`, `viewUrl` (The video's page, from that moment), `embedUrl` (The chrome-less player for an iframe, from that moment).
