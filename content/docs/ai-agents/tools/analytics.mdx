Views over time, where they came from, and every view. 6 tools.

## `get_video_activity`

**Every plan.** Reads only. What viewers did with a video: its view count, where views came from (viewsBy: sources such as Direct, Google or X, link page vs embeds, top countries), views per UTC day over the last days (daily, oldest first), likes, and every comment (oldest first, with author and time).

**Parameters**

| Parameter | Type | Required | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `videoId` | string | yes | The video's id (videoId from list_videos). |
| `days` | integer | no | How many days back to count views per day (default 30). |


**Returns** `videoId`, `views`, `viewsBy` (Where views came from; null when they couldn't be counted just now (get_video_activity: or it has no views yet)), `daily`, `likes`, `likedByUser`, `comments`, `viewUrl` (The video's page on recordful.app), `editUrl` (Where the user edits it).

## `get_analytics`

**Every plan.** Reads only. How the user's whole library is watched: total views and views per UTC day over the last days (default 30), plus the ten most viewed videos in that time with links. Covers the videos the user can see, not those in trash. get_video_activity has one video's detail.

**Parameters**

| Parameter | Type | Required | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `days` | integer | no | How many days back to count views per day (default 30). |


**Returns** `days`, `views`, `daily`, `topVideos`.

## `get_video_analytics`

**Pro.** Reads only. How one video was watched over the last days (default 30): views in that time and all time, views per UTC day, where they came from (sources, referring sites, UTM tags, link page vs embeds, countries), how far plays got (retention: plays, watch time, average share watched, drop-off curve), plus likes and comments. A view is one viewer opening the video's page or playing its embed; the same viewer again within 24 hours isn't counted again. Recordful doesn't record who watched (viewers stay anonymous) or call-to-action clicks.

**Parameters**

| Parameter | Type | Required | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `videoId` | string | yes | The video's id (videoId from list_videos). |
| `days` | integer | no | How many days back to count views per day (default 30). |


**Returns** `videoId`, `days`, `views` (Views in the range), `totalViews` (Views since it was made), `daily`, `viewsBy` (Where views came from; null when they couldn't be counted just now (get_video_activity: or it has no views yet)), `retention` (Null while nobody has played it in the range), `likes`, `comments`, `viewUrl` (The video's page on recordful.app), `editUrl` (Where the user edits it).
