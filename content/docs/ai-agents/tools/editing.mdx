Read how a video is edited and change it in one atomic batch. 2 tools.

## `get_timeline`

**Pro.** Reads only. How a Recordful video is edited, as it plays now: its clips (with speed and where each comes from in the recording), zooms, captions and their style, markers, motion layers, camera layout, frame and background, audio settings, and the edit's version. Every time is in seconds of the edited video, the times apply_video_edits takes. Only the person who recorded it, or the workspace owner, can.

**Parameters**

| Parameter | Type | Required | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `videoId` | string | yes | The video's id (videoId from list_videos). |


**Returns** `videoId`, `version` (The edit's version. Pass it as expectedVersion to apply edits only to the edit you read), `durationSeconds` (How long the video plays as edited), `clips`, `zooms`, `zoomDepth`, `captions`, `markers`, `camera`, `frame`, `audio`, `motion`, `cursor`, `masks` (Blurs and spotlights), `shapes` (Arrows, boxes and highlights), `viewUrl` (The video's page on recordful.app), `editUrl` (Where the user edits it).

## `apply_video_edits`

**Pro.** Changes something. Apply 1 to 200 edits to a Recordful video in one call, in order, each on the edit the one before left: cut_range, split_clip, reorder_clip, delete_clip, duplicate_clip, set_clip_speed, set_clip_transition, cut_words, add_zoom, update_zoom, remove_zoom, remove_silences, remove_zooms, generate_auto_zooms, add_shape, update_shape, remove_shape, add_mask, update_mask, remove_mask, set_watermark, remove_captions, set_caption_style, add_captions, update_transcript_words, remove_fillers, set_frame, set_background, set_aspect_ratio, set_camera, set_cursor_effects, set_volumes, set_voice_enhancement, mute_range, unmute_range, make_motion, edit_motion_doc, remove_motion, add_video_effect, edit_video_effect, remove_video_effect, set_camera_background. All or nothing: one invalid operation (invalid_argument names it) and nothing applies. Times are seconds of the video as it plays now (get_timeline), so after a cut_range later times move earlier. Pass expectedVersion from get_timeline to apply only to the edit you read (required with ops that name a clip or zoom by index); edit_conflict means it changed meanwhile (the person may be editing it) and nothing applied: read it again and retry. The recording's files never change, so every edit can be undone in the editor. Only the person who recorded it, or the workspace owner, can.

#### `make_motion`

Make a graphic from motion document commands and put it on the video as a motion layer from start to end (it runs longer if its parts need it). Times inside commands are seconds from the layer's start: a word said at 12.4 s in a layer starting at 11.9 s is at 0.5. Graphic commands (x, y, sizes in canvas pixels; the canvas is the video's shape, its size in list_motion; colours #rrggbb): - add_chart &#123;type: bar|column|line|area|pie|donut|stat|progress|comparison, items:[&#123;label, value&#125;], title?, highlight? (label; null for none), format? &#123;prefix, suffix, decimals&#125;, accent?, theme? dark|light, background? (false floats it over the video), steps?&#125; - add_diagram &#123;type: flowchart|pipeline|timeline|cycle, text: "Record -> Trim -> Share" (flowchart: "Valid? -yes-> Save" labels an arrow, one chain a line), title?, accent?, background?, steps?&#125; - add_title &#123;text, style: slam|knockout, x?, y?, color?&#125;. Knockout letters are filled with the video: pass blend "knockout". - add_kinetic_words &#123;words:[&#123;text, at&#125;], color?, background? (a colour behind them)&#125;: words that land one at a time round the speaker. - add_hud &#123;text? ("REC"), label?, color?, grid?, scan?&#125;: corner ticks and a status line over the picture. - add_arrow &#123;from:[x,y], to:[x,y], label?, curve?, color?&#125;; add_highlight &#123;x, y, width?, height?, shape: circle|box, label?, dim?&#125;: point at or circle something on screen. - add_icon &#123;name (a lucide icon, see list_motion_options), x?, y?, size?, color?, in?&#125;; add_illustration &#123;parts:[icon names], highlight?&#125;; add_logo &#123;text, style?&#125;. - Low level: add_layer &#123;layer:&#123;type: text|shape|path|image|group, ...&#125;&#125;, update_layer &#123;id, set&#125;, remove_layer &#123;id&#125;, add_keyframes &#123;id, property, keyframes:[&#123;t, v&#125;]&#125;, animate_text, add_effect, set_canvas &#123;background&#125;. Charts and diagrams sit on a full-frame card that hides the video unless background is false. steps lands each bar or diagram node on the word that names it: one time per item, in order, seconds from the layer's start. When the items are said, always pass steps. The canvas is 1920 by 1080. To point at something on screen, see it first with get_video_frame (its pixels times 1920 / its width are canvas pixels, taking the recording as filling the canvas).
