---
title: Connect an AI app with no install
description: "Add Recordful to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or any app that connects to a remote MCP server. It can find and read your videos, and with Recordful Pro edit, organise and share them."
---

The hosted MCP server lets an AI app work with your Recordful library without installing anything on your computer. It can find and read your videos on every plan. With Recordful Pro it can also edit them (cuts, silences, filler words, zooms, captions, look, audio, overlays), look at them first (frames and storyboards), organise and share them, and read their analytics. To have an AI app record your browser, use the [local server](/docs/ai-agents/setup) instead.

## Add it

The address is the same in every app:

```
https://recordful.app/mcp
```

- **Claude (claude.ai and the desktop app):** Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, paste the address.
- **Claude Code:** `claude mcp add --transport http recordful https://recordful.app/mcp`
- **ChatGPT:** Settings → Connectors → Create, paste the address (developer mode).
- **Cursor, VS Code and others:** add a remote (Streamable HTTP) MCP server with the address.

The app sends you to recordful.app to sign in. It asks to **read your videos** and, separately, to **change your videos and folders**. Approve only what you want it to do: without the second, the tools that change things are refused.

Then ask things like "cut the silences and ums out of yesterday's demo", "make a 30-second vertical clip of the part about pricing", "add captions and zoom in on every click", or "which of my videos got the most views this month".

It sees what you can see in your workspace, nothing more. Only the person who recorded a video, or the workspace owner, can edit it.

## How edits stay safe

- **Nothing is lost.** Edits change how the video plays, never the recording. Everything an AI does shows in Recordful's editor, where you can change or undo it.
- **Versions.** Reading a timeline gives a `version`. An edit sent with `expectedVersion` applies only if nobody changed the video meanwhile; otherwise it answers `edit_conflict` and changes nothing. Tools that pick something by its number (a clip, a zoom, a shape) always need it.
- **All or nothing.** `apply_video_edits` takes up to 200 changes and applies all of them or none.
- **Retries are safe.** A call sent again with the same `_meta.idempotencyKey` within 24 hours returns the first answer instead of doing it twice.
- **Typed errors.** Every failure has a code (`not_found`, `forbidden`, `invalid_argument`, `edit_conflict`, `not_ready`, `rate_limited` and others) and says whether trying again can help.
- **Limits.** 200 tool calls a minute per person in each workspace.

## Remove an app

Open **Settings** at recordful.app. Under **Connected AI apps**, each app shows what it may do and when it was last used. **Remove** ends its access at once; its next request is refused.
