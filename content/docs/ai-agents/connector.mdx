---
title: Connect an AI app with no install
description: "Add Recordful to Claude or any app that connects to a remote MCP server, sign in, and it can read your library: videos, transcripts, summaries and views."
---

The hosted connector lets an AI app read your Recordful library without installing anything on your computer. It is **read-only**: it can look things up, never change or delete them. To have an AI app record your browser or manage videos, use the [local server](/docs/ai-agents/setup) instead.

## Add it

1. In your AI app, add a custom connector (or "remote MCP server") with this address:

   ```
   https://recordful.app/mcp
   ```

2. The app sends you to recordful.app to sign in. Check what it asks for, **Can read your videos**, and approve.

That's it. Ask things like "find the video where I demo the billing page", "what did I say about pricing in last week's update", or "which of my videos got the most views this month".

## What it can do

| Tool | What it reads |
| --- | --- |
| `list_videos` | Your videos, newest first, or by a search |
| `get_video` | One video: its links, length and views |
| `get_transcript` | What was said, or the moments a phrase comes up |
| `get_summary` | The AI summary and chapters |
| `get_video_activity` | Views over time and where they came from |
| `get_analytics` | Views across your workspace |
| `list_folders` | Your folders |

It sees what you can see in your workspace, nothing more.

## Remove an app

Open **Settings** at recordful.app. Under **Connected AI apps**, each app shows what it may do and when it was last used. **Remove** ends its access at once; its next request is refused.
