---
title: Share a recording with a link
description: Choose who can watch, send the link, start it at a moment, or embed it.
---

Every cloud recording has a link: `recordful.app/view/<id>`. Who can open it depends on its **Who can watch** setting.

| Who can watch | Who can open the link |
| --- | --- |
| Private | Only you |
| Shared with workspace only | Signed-in members of your workspace |
| Public | Anyone with the link |

To change it, open the recording (you need to have made it, or own the workspace), click **⋯** next to its title and pick **Who can watch**, or use **Share** under the player. A change applies right away: moving a public recording back to your workspace or to private stops the link working for everyone else.

## Invite people by email

To let one person watch without making the recording public, open **Share** and add their email under **People with access**. They get an email with a link, even if they are outside your workspace. Invited people can watch whatever **Who can watch** says, past a link password or an end date, and can comment and like. Remove someone from the same list and their access ends.

## Private by default

Workspace owners and admins can make every new recording start as **Private**: open **Workspace settings** and turn on **New recordings are private**. It applies to recordings from the extension, the desktop app and uploads. Change any one recording's setting afterwards as usual.

## Link options

Add these to the end of a link:

- `?t=90` starts playing 90 seconds in.
- `?captions=off` starts with captions hidden. Viewers can still turn them on with **CC** or the **c** key.

Use both with `&`, for example `recordful.app/view/<id>?t=90&captions=off`.

## Add a call to action

Finish a video with a button that sends viewers somewhere: book a call, start a trial, read the docs. Click **⋯** next to the title and pick **Call to action**, then set a headline, the button's label, its link and colour, and where it shows: at the **end** of the video, as a small **card in the corner** while it plays, or both. Viewers need nothing to see it. Part of Pro.

## Replace the video, keep the link

Recorded it again? Click **⋯** next to the title and pick **Replace video…** to upload the new file behind the same link. The old file, its edit, transcript and summary go; the link you already sent keeps working.

## Edit the summary and chapters

In the **Summary** tab, click **Edit** to rewrite the AI summary or the chapters, add a chapter at the playhead, or clear them all. With no summary yet, **Write one yourself**. Your version is kept even if an AI summary finishes later, and chapters show as marks on the player's scrubber.

## Embed a recording

A public recording plays on other sites at `recordful.app/embed/<id>`, for example in an `<iframe>`. Add `?autoplay=1` to start playing as soon as it loads (browsers may still ask for a click). The link options above work here too.

## Views and downloads

Views count people outside your workspace who open the link. The person who made the recording, and the workspace owner, see where they came from under **⋯ > Analytics**.

Only members of your workspace can download a recording. Viewers outside it can watch (and, when signed in, comment and like), but don't see **Download**.
