---
title: Share a recording with a link
description: Choose who can watch, send the link, start it at a moment, or embed it.
---

Every cloud recording has a link: `recordful.app/view/<id>`. Who can open it depends on its **Who can watch** setting.

| Who can watch | Who can open the link |
| --- | --- |
| Private | Only you |
| Shared with workspace only | Signed-in members of your workspace |
| Public | Anyone with the link |

To change it, open the recording (you need to have made it, or own the workspace), click **⋯** next to its title and pick **Who can watch**, or use **Share** under the player. A change applies right away: moving a public recording back to your workspace or to private stops the link working for everyone else.

## Link options

Add these to the end of a link:

- `?t=90` starts playing 90 seconds in.
- `?captions=off` hides the captions.

Use both with `&`, for example `recordful.app/view/<id>?t=90&captions=off`.

## Embed a recording

A public recording plays on other sites at `recordful.app/embed/<id>`, for example in an `<iframe>`. Add `?autoplay=1` to start playing as soon as it loads (browsers may still ask for a click). The link options above work here too.

## Views and downloads

Views count people outside your workspace who open the link. The person who made the recording, and the workspace owner, see where they came from under **⋯ > Analytics**.

Only members of your workspace can download a recording. Viewers outside it can watch (and, when signed in, comment and like), but don't see **Download**.
