---
title: Share a recording with a link
description: Turn on a share link, send it, and turn it off when done.
---

With Pro, open the recording on the dashboard and enable its share link. Anyone with the link can watch until you disable it. Recordings are private to your workspace by default.

<MediaPlaceholder kind="screenshot" label="Dashboard share toggle and copy-link button" />

If a link should not be public anymore, turn the toggle off. The old URL stops working immediately.
