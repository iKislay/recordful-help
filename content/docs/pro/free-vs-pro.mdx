---
title: Free vs Pro
description: What is free forever locally and what Pro adds in the cloud.
---

Free, no account: unlimited tab, screen, camera, and area recordings, live annotation, camera effects, built-in trim, crop, mute, MP4, WebM, and GIF export. Shapes, markers, transitions, style presets and crash recovery are free too. Recordings stay in your browser.

Pro ($19/month or $108/year, 14-day trial on yearly) adds: cloud storage with share links, multi-scene editing, auto-zoom on clicks, captions, [editing by transcript](/docs/editing/transcript), the [Voice EQ](/docs/editing/voice-eq) and voice clean-up, calls to action and viewer invites on [share pages](/docs/pro/share-links), the [Loom importer](/docs/pro/loom-import), and the [Recordful Agent](/docs/editing/agent) with 4,500 [AI credits](/docs/pro/ai-credits) a month. It supports indie builder Kislay and covers storage and transcription costs.

Full terms are on [recordful.app/pro](https://recordful.app/pro).
