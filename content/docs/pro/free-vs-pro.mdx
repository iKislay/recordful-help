---
title: Free vs Pro
description: What is free forever locally and what Pro adds in the cloud.
---

Free, no account: unlimited tab, screen, camera, and area recordings, live annotation, camera effects, built-in trim, crop, mute, MP4, WebM, and GIF export. Recordings stay in your browser.

Pro ($13/month or $120/year, 2-week trial on yearly) adds: cloud storage with share links, multi-scene editing, auto-zoom on clicks, and captions. It supports indie builder Kislay and covers storage and transcription costs.

Full terms are on [recordful.app/pro](https://recordful.app/pro).
