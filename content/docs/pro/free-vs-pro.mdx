---
title: Free vs Pro
description: What is free forever locally and what Pro adds in the cloud.
---

Free, no account: unlimited tab, screen, camera, and area recordings, live annotation, camera effects, built-in trim, crop, mute, auto-zoom on clicks, MP4, WebM, and GIF export. Free recordings in Chrome are up to 1080p, and free exports are up to 2K. Shapes, markers, transitions, style presets and crash recovery are free too. Recordings stay in your browser.

AI features run on [AI credits](/docs/pro/ai-credits), with or without Pro: the [Recordful Agent](/docs/editing/agent), captions, [editing by transcript](/docs/editing/transcript), voice clean-up and the [Voice EQ](/docs/editing/voice-eq). A new account gets 100 credits free.

Pro ($19/month or $108/year, 14-day trial on yearly) adds: 4K recording and export, cloud storage with share links, multi-scene editing, 3D perspective on zooms, shortening long pauses, calls to action and viewer invites on [share pages](/docs/pro/share-links), the [Loom importer](/docs/pro/loom-import), and 3,500 [AI credits](/docs/pro/ai-credits) a month for the workspace. It supports indie builder Kislay and covers storage and transcription costs.

Full terms are on [recordful.app/pro](https://recordful.app/pro).
