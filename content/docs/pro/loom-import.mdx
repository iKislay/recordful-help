title: Move your videos from Loom
description: "Paste Loom share links or upload Loom's CSV export, and Recordful copies each video into your library with its title."
---

Moving from Loom doesn't mean starting over. The importer copies your Loom videos into your Recordful library, where they get share links, transcripts and summaries like any recording. Part of [Recordful Pro](/docs/pro/free-vs-pro).

## Import

1. Sign in at [recordful.app](https://recordful.app) and click **Import** in the dashboard's sidebar.
2. Paste one or more Loom share links (`loom.com/share/…`), one per line, or upload the **CSV export** from your Loom library.
3. Start the import. Each video shows its own progress: **Waiting**, **Copying**, then **In your library**.

You can close the page: the copying runs on our side, and the page shows where it got to when you come back. Pasting the same link twice imports it once.

## If a video doesn't come across

- **Retry** restarts a video that failed or got stuck.
- If Loom won't share a video (it is private, or Loom blocks the download), download it from Loom yourself and click **Upload MP4** on that row.

## Good to know

- Only videos you can open with their share link can be copied.
- Imported videos count toward your [storage](/docs/pro/storage) like any upload.
