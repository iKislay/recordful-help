title: AI credits
description: "How AI credits work: 100 free when you sign up, 3,500 a month with Pro, shared by everyone in the workspace."
---

Recordful's AI features spend AI credits. Credits belong to the workspace, so everyone in it spends from the same balance.

- **100 free credits when you sign up.** A new account gets them once, in its own workspace, with or without Pro.
- **3,500 credits a month with Pro.** They arrive on your billing day and don't carry over. They're spent before your sign-up credits.
- **See what's left** at the top of the [Agent](/docs/editing/agent)'s sidebar.

## What spends credits

- **The [Recordful Agent](/docs/editing/agent):** by how much work a request takes. A typical request spends about 1; one with many edits spends more.
- **Captions, filler words, [editing by transcript](/docs/editing/transcript) and video transcripts:** about 4 credits a minute of sound.
- **AI summaries and chapters:** 1 or 2 each.
- **Voice clean-up** (noise and echo, mouth sounds, breaths): 1 credit per started minute of a take, once. Turning it off and on again, or exporting again, spends nothing more.
- **The [Voice EQ](/docs/editing/voice-eq)** needs credits or Pro to turn on, but spends none.

Every paid request spends at least 1 credit.

## When they run out

With Pro, AI features pause until your billing day; everything else keeps working. Without Pro, [Recordful Pro](/docs/pro/free-vs-pro) adds 3,500 a month. Credits can't be bought separately yet.
