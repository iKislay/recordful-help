---
title: AI credits
description: "How the Recordful Agent's AI credits work: 4,500 a month for each Pro workspace, shared by everyone in it."
---

AI features like the [Recordful Agent](/docs/editing/agent) spend AI credits. Credits are part of Pro.

- **4,500 credits a month** for each Pro workspace, shared by everyone in it.
- **A typical request spends around 10.** What a request spends depends on how much work it takes: a quick question spends a few, a request with several edits spends more. Every request spends at least 1, including one you stop partway.
- **They renew on the 1st of each month.** Credits left over don't carry over.
- **See what's left** at the top of the agent's sidebar. When they run out, the sidebar says when they renew, and the agent pauses until then. Everything else in Recordful keeps working.

Credits can't be bought separately yet.
