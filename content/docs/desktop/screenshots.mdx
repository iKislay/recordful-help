The desktop app (2.0.0 or newer) takes screenshots from anywhere on your computer and opens them in the same screenshot editor as recordful.app.

## Take a screenshot

Start one any of these ways:

- Press **Ctrl+Alt+S** (**⌘⌥S** on a Mac), from any app, even with Recordful's windows closed. You can [change the shortcut](#change-the-shortcut).
- Click the camera button on the floating toolbar.
- **File → Take Screenshot**.
- **Take Screenshot** in Recordful's tray menu (the menu bar on a Mac).
- **Library → Screenshots → New screenshot**.

The screen freezes first, so open menus and hover states stay in the shot. Then:

- **Click a window** to take just that window.
- **Click the desktop** to take the whole screen.
- **Drag an area.** It's taken when you let go.
- Press **Esc** to cancel.

It works across monitors. On a Mac it needs the **Screen Recording** permission, the same one recording uses (see [First launch on a Mac](/docs/getting-started/requirements#first-launch-on-a-mac)).

## Always ready

Closing Recordful's windows doesn't quit it: it keeps running in the system tray (the menu bar on a Mac), so the shortcut works any time. The first time, a notification tells you where it went. To quit, open the tray menu and choose **Quit**.

Recordful also **opens at login**, quietly into the tray with no window, so the shortcut works right after you start your computer. Turn this off in **Settings → Recording → Open at login**.

## Change the shortcut

Open **Settings → Recording → Screenshot shortcut**, click the shortcut, and press the keys you want: at least one of Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) or Alt with a key, or an F-key or Print Screen on its own. **Esc** cancels and **Reset** goes back to Ctrl+Alt+S.

If another app or the system already uses those keys, Recordful says so and keeps the shortcut you had. The toolbar's tooltip, the menus and the Library always show the shortcut you chose.

## Where it goes

As soon as you take it:

1. The picture is copied to the clipboard, so you can paste it straight away.
2. It's saved as a PNG in **Pictures/Recordful**, named like `Screenshot 2026-11-12 at 20.31.04.png`.
3. It opens in its own window with the screenshot editor.

## Edit it

The editor is the same one as on recordful.app:

- **Mark up:** arrows, shapes, pen, highlighter, text, numbered steps, spotlight, blur, pixelate and crop.
- **Style:** looks, wallpapers, frames, shadow, 3D tilt and a watermark.
- **Export** as PNG, JPEG or WebP at 0.5× to 3×, or **copy** it.
- **Share to X, Threads or Forg.** Recordful copies the picture and opens the post page. Paste it there.

Edits are saved on your computer as you go. The PNG in Pictures/Recordful is never changed, so the original is always there. The **Library** button in the editor takes you back to the app's Library.

## The starting look

A new screenshot starts on a wallpaper photo picked to match its colours: a blue page gets a blue wallpaper, a white page a soft lilac one, a dark editor a dark one. It's always a photo, never a gradient, with rounder corners. The web editor, the Chrome extension, the desktop app and [AI agents](/docs/ai-agents) all start this way.

If you turned on **Remember my look**, your saved look is used instead.

## Find it again

**Library → Screenshots** shows every screenshot as you last edited it. From there you can open one, show it in its folder, or move it to the trash. To add a picture you already have, paste it or drop it onto the Screenshots page.

## In the cloud (Pro)

With [Recordful Pro](/docs/pro/free-vs-pro), each screenshot is also kept in your cloud **Screenshots** folder. It's private until you share it. **Get link** gives you a link to share; if you're signed out it asks you to sign in first, and on the free plan it opens the upgrade page.

Cloud screenshots show in the Library too, and open in the app's editor.
